# 💎 Crystal Capital DAO

**Official documentation of the decentralized investment fund.**

Crystal Capital DAO transitions capital management in digital assets (BTC, ETH, SOL) and RWA (Real World Assets) from the realm of intuitive speculation to strict statistical production. Our architecture eliminates human emotion, relying exclusively on mathematical expectancy and structural liquidity imbalances.

## Fundamental Principles

1. **Anti-fragility:** Reducing the probability of capital ruin to absolute zero via The Zero-Ruin Protocol (strict 0.5% risk per trade).
2. **Mathematical Edge:** Exploiting the Mean Reversion strategy in zones of retail cascading liquidations.
3. **Meritocracy:** Capital governance through Soulbound Crystal Reputation Tokens (CRT), where a participant's influence is proportional to their proven efficiency, not their deposit size.
4. **Industrial Scaling:** A distributed infrastructure of independent sub-accounts to ensure the purity of statistical sampling.
